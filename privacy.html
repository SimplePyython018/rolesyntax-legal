Privacy at a Glance

RoleSyntax is designed to process only the information reasonably necessary to provide its enabled features, maintain the service, and support server functionality.

01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Hidden Team ("Hidden Team", "we", "us", or "our") may process information through RoleSyntax ("RoleSyntax" or "the Bot").

RoleSyntax is a Discord bot designed to provide role selection, role management, automation, and related server functionality.

By using RoleSyntax, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled by a Discord server, RoleSyntax may process information made available through Discord and information necessary for the Bot to perform its functions.

This may include:

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord server or guild ID
  • Username or display name
  • Role IDs and role-related selections
  • Server configuration or Bot settings
  • Interaction information needed to respond to commands or buttons
  • Technical information necessary to operate and maintain the service

The exact information processed may depend on the features enabled and how RoleSyntax is configured by a server administrator.

03

How Information Is Used

Information processed by RoleSyntax may be used for purposes such as:

  • Providing role selection and role-management features
  • Responding to commands, buttons, menus, or other interactions
  • Saving necessary server configuration
  • Maintaining and improving Bot functionality
  • Monitoring technical errors and service problems
  • Protecting the service from abuse or unauthorized activity
  • Providing support when a problem is reported

Hidden Team does not intend to process information beyond what is reasonably necessary for the operation of RoleSyntax and its enabled features.

04

Discord Information

RoleSyntax operates through Discord and may receive certain information that Discord makes available to applications and bots.

Such information may include identifiers, usernames, display names, server information, role information, interaction data, and other information made available through Discord's platform and permission system.

Your use of Discord is separately governed by Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

05

Data Storage and Retention

Some information may be stored when necessary for RoleSyntax to remember configuration, provide persistent functionality, troubleshoot technical issues, or maintain the service.

The amount and duration of storage may vary depending on the feature, server configuration, technical requirements, and operational needs of the service.

Hidden Team aims to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is used, subject to technical, operational, security, and legal requirements.

Important

Removing RoleSyntax from a server may stop future processing for that server, but may not immediately remove information that was already stored for legitimate operational purposes.

06

Information Sharing

Hidden Team does not sell personal information collected or processed through RoleSyntax.

Information may only be accessed, disclosed, or processed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate and maintain RoleSyntax
  • Provide requested support
  • Protect the service and its users
  • Use infrastructure or service providers required to operate the Bot
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations
  • Investigate abuse, security incidents, or technical problems

Hidden Team will not intentionally use RoleSyntax to collect unnecessary personal information.

07

Security

Hidden Team takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information processed through RoleSyntax.

However, no online service or software system can guarantee absolute security. Users and server administrators are also responsible for maintaining the security of their own Discord accounts, permissions, and server configuration.

08

Third-Party Services

RoleSyntax relies on Discord and may rely on third-party hosting, infrastructure, databases, APIs, monitoring, or other technical services.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Hidden Team is not responsible for privacy practices or security incidents caused by third-party services outside its reasonable control.

09

Your Choices

Depending on the feature involved, users and server administrators may control certain information by changing their Discord settings, server configuration, or by discontinuing use of RoleSyntax.

Server administrators may remove RoleSyntax from their Discord server at any time.

For questions regarding stored information or a privacy concern related to RoleSyntax, contact Hidden Team through the official support channel.

10

Children and Age Requirements

RoleSyntax is a Discord application and does not independently determine a user's age.

Users must meet the age and account requirements established by Discord and applicable law in their location.

11

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Hidden Team may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to RoleSyntax, its features, technical infrastructure, security practices, or applicable requirements.

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this Privacy Policy was most recently revised.

Continued use of RoleSyntax after changes become effective constitutes acknowledgment of the updated Privacy Policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

12

Contact

For privacy questions, concerns, data-related requests, or other issues involving RoleSyntax, contact Hidden Team through the official RoleSyntax support server.

Please provide enough information for the support team to understand and investigate your request.
